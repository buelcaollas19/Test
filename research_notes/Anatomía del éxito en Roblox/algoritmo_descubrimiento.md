## 1. How does Roblox's Home recommendation system work according to Roblox itself (signals, pipeline, cold start, sorts, 2025–2026 changes, Charts)?

### Takeaway
As of Nov 2026 Roblox officially describes "Recommended for You" (RFY) as a two-stage retrieval → ranking system, personalised per user, fed by eight named per-user signals measured only on users organically acquired from RFY, over Day 1 / Day 2–7 / Day 8–28 windows (the 28-day horizon was added in June 2026; the older "qPTR + D1/D7 + playtime + payer conversion" description from 2024–2025 is superseded). 2026 added Deep play-through rate, a 7-day qualified-sessions signal, dismissal feedback, video previews, labelled ads inside RFY, a mandatory custom thumbnail (Oct 2026), age-aware ranking and a "direct growth" test (RDC 2026), and the Moments video feed as a new discovery surface.

### Cited Findings

**A. Current official description of the pipeline (Creator Hub "Discovery" doc + "Discovery FAQ", read Nov 2026)**
- [DOC] RFY works in two stages. *Retrieval*: "the algorithm selects a subset of games that each user might enjoy playing based on key signals like engagement, retention, and monetization." *Ranking*: chooses the most relevant of those games and orders them personally for each user. — [Creator Hub: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery) (mirror: [GitHub](https://github.com/Roblox/creator-docs/blob/main/content/en-us/discovery.md))
- [DOC] The pre-ranking list is per user: the system "starts with all eligible games and narrows to about 100 games on a user's Home page", personalising at each stage, and "continuously explores new games with new users". — [Creator Hub: Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq) (mirror: [GitHub raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Roblox/creator-docs/main/content/en-us/discovery-faq.md))
- [DOC] "Explore and expand" phases: in *explore* the system tests the game with new users (e.g. a spike of new users from recommendations after a content update); if that cohort engages and monetises well, the system keeps recommending the game to similar cohorts (*expand*). — [Creator Hub: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] Home impressions depend on four factors: (1) your own updates/gameplay changes, (2) Roblox's algorithm changes, (3) total Home traffic with seasonality (weekly peak on Saturday; summer, back-to-school, holidays), (4) competitor performance — "a 50% signal gain can yield minimal impression growth if competitors improve more." — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] Stated objective: discovery aims at **long-term retention and long-term monetization**; "no single signal determines ranking". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)

**B. The eight named RFY signals (official, current)** — [Creator Hub: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)

| Tier | Signal | Official definition | Windows / caps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Most important | **Play through rate (PTR)** | "The rate at which users play your game after seeing it in the Recommended for You sort." | first impression/session |
| Most important | **First play bounce rate** (negative signal) | "The rate at which users leave your game after a short play session." | two buckets: <60 s and 61–180 s |
| Most important | **Play days per user** | "The average number of unique days users engage with your game." | Day 1, Day 2–7, Day 8–28 |
| Most important | **Playtime per user** | "The average amount of time users spend in your game." | Day 1, Day 2–7, Day 8–28; **capped at 60 minutes per user, per game, per day** |
| Important | **Intentional co-play days per user** | "The average number of unique days that users come back to play your game with friends" — includes join, invite and private-server co-play | D1 / D2–7 / D8–28 |
| Important | **Qualified play sessions per user** | meaningful sessions that filter out accidental clicks and quick bounces | D1 / D2–7 / D8–28 |
| Important | **Spend days per user** | "The average number of unique days users spend Robux in your game." | D1 / D2–7 / D8–28 |
| Important | **Robux spent per user** | "The average amount of Robux users spend in your game." | D1 / D2–7 / D8–28 |

- [DOC] All signals are **per-user averages, not totals**, so "smaller games with engaged users aren't disadvantaged"; larger games are not favoured. — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] Signals count **only users organically acquired via RFY**. Engagement/retention/monetisation of users first acquired via ads, curation, friends, search or social media is *not* counted in the RFY ranking stage. PTR and bounce rate measure only the first session of RFY-acquired users; returning traffic from "Continue" does not count as RFY impressions; D2–7 and D8–28 metrics do include later returns of RFY-acquired users. — [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery); [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] Day 2–7 play days = how many of the 6 days in the window users played on average; playtime shows depth, play days shows frequency. The algorithm considers the D1, D2–7 and D8–28 windows "each separately and together". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] Genre and gameplay type "aren't explicit ranking factors"; the algorithm learns from behaviour; changing a genre tag does not change behavioural signals. Story-driven/finite games are not penalised for lower long-term retention. — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] A game strong in only retention *or* monetisation still receives Home impressions, but "games strong in both areas may get broader distribution". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, early/March 2026] Roblox added **Deep play through rate** to RFY: where qPTR "measures the funnel conversion from impression to qualified play and filters out accidental clicks and quick bounces", Deep PTR targets "sustained interest and active participation"; visible in Creator Analytics > Acquisition > Home Recommendations. Exact formula not published (forum users only guess "qPTR with a higher playtime bar"). — [DevForum: How we are improving Home this year](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571); developer summary: [Mia Gobble on X](https://x.com/iGottic_Real/status/2031045051409658129)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, 2026] A **"7-day qualified play sessions"** signal was added to RFY, and **user dismissal feedback** ("not interested") now feeds discovery relevance. A DevForum excerpt says a qualified session "typically means 5+ minutes of engagement" — note this 5-minute figure is NOT in the Creator Hub docs, which only say "meaningful session". — [DevForum: How we are improving Home this year](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571)

**C. Cold start / new and small experiences (official)**
- [DOC] No 28-day wait for new games: "Early signals apply immediately" — PTR and bounce rate (first session) → Day 1 signals (playtime, play days, Robux spend, co-play) → Day 2–7 → Day 8–28; "more data improves targeting over time". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] Retrieval "needs only a very low minimum number of plays from any source" (number not published). Ranking then "tests the game with a small user set, monitors retention daily, and increases impressions if performance improves". Old games can regrow the same way. — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] A weak start is not a permanent penalty: signals use a moving window; after fixes "the algorithm keeps testing with new users daily"; if they don't retain "it recalibrates and tests different users". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] PTR "may dip temporarily when impressions rise, then stabilize" (exploration into colder audiences). — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, 19 Oct 2026 per secondary dating] **Hard eligibility rule:** "Homepage Recommendation Eligibility Now Requires a Custom Thumbnail" — games with only the default/auto-generated thumbnail receive no Home recommendation impressions; AI-generated images count as custom; 16:9, ideally 1920×1080. The Creator Hub Discovery doc confirms: "A custom thumbnail is required… to receive impressions from homepage recommendations". — [DevForum announcement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/homepage-recommendation-eligibility-now-requires-a-custom-thumbnail/4945086); [Discovery doc](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)

### Inferences
- The widely circulated formula "qPTR + D1 + D7 + D7 playtime + payer conversion" describes the Dec 2024–2025 system. The Nov 2026 system is better summarised as: PTR + bounce (<60 s, 61–180 s) + play days + playtime (60-min/day cap) as first-order signals, with co-play days, qualified sessions, spend days and Robux/user as second-order, each evaluated across D1 / D2–7 / D8–28 and only on the RFY-acquired cohort.
- Because the playtime signal is capped at 60 min/user/day and play *days* is a top-tier signal, the algorithm rewards frequency of return (many days) over marathon sessions.
- Because only RFY-acquired users count, ads/TikTok traffic cannot directly "feed" RFY signals; they can only help indirectly (see Q5).
- Personalisation by age is now explicit (RDC 2026 "age-aware" statement). Region/language/device personalisation is implied by features (Charts filter by country/device, semantic search in supported languages, thumbnail personalisation by user group, ads targeting by location/age/gender/genre/device) but no official doc reviewed states how these enter RFY ranking.

## 2. What benchmarks do Roblox analytics dashboards give for impressions → CTR → play-through → retention, and how do "benchmarks vs similar experiences" work?

### Takeaway
Roblox publishes *how* benchmarks are built (50th–90th percentile range of games with similar players, fallback to genre, then all experiences; Top 200/500/1000 tiers) but publishes no universal "good" PTR/CTR number; the only official numeric example is a D1 retention benchmark range of 12.11%–18.73%. Numeric qPTR figures in circulation (≈2–2.4% typical, ≈3.6% top) are third-party.

### Cited Findings
- [DOC] Benchmark set, in fallback order: (1) **games with similar players** ("if the model finds enough games enjoyed by players that are similar to yours"), (2) **genre** (if not enough similar games but the game has an internal genre), (3) **all experiences** (if no genre label yet). Benchmark games must have **≥100 DAU**; similar-game benchmarks update daily; charts are annotated when the benchmark set changes. — [Creator Hub: Analytics dashboard](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard)
- [DOC] Similar-game KPIs show the **50th–90th percentile range**. Official example: Day 1 Retention range 12.11%–18.73%, read as "50% of games with similar players have a Day 1 Retention of 12.11% or lower" (illustrative example, not a target). — [Analytics dashboard](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard)
- [DOC] KPIs with similar-game benchmarks: all Retention KPIs; Average Session Time; ARPPU, ARPDAU, Conversion Rate (payer CVR); Acquisition **Play Through Rate**. — [Analytics dashboard](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard)
- [DOC] "Overall success" benchmarks: top 1,000 games by total playtime over a rolling 30 days (excluding games <30 days old), tiers **Top 200 / Top 500 / Top 1000**. Dashboard access requires >10 DAU and 10 play hours for 7 consecutive days. — [Analytics dashboard](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard)
- [DOC] Benchmarks "do not affect the Recommended for You algorithm in any way"; they are only a comparison point; the benchmark group changes as the game scales. — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq); [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] Acquisition dashboard (Analytics > Acquisition): charts for new/returning users with plays and with impressions per source, Play through rate, cumulative new-users funnel; attribution metrics per source: users with plays, **conversion rate** ("percent of new users who played your game after viewing an impression"), **D7 retention** ("percentage of new players acquired in the date range who played again on the 8th day after their first session ended"), **7D playtime per user**, **30D payer conversion**, **30D revenue per user** (excl. subscriptions, engagement payouts, immersive ads). — [Creator Hub: Acquisition](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)
- [DOC] Home Recommendations tab (Analytics > Acquisition): shows the RFY signal charts; "impression drops usually follow declines in play through rate, play days, and playtime, or a rise in first play bounce rate". — [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] Retention definitions: D1 = new users returning the next day; D7 = returning on the 8th day; D30 = returning on the 31st day; cohort views daily (first 10 days) and weekly (10 weeks), with 7D playtime/user, 7D payer conversion, 7D and 30D revenue/user. Engagement dashboard has a "New User First Session Retention" chart (share of new users still playing X minutes after joining). — [Creator Hub: Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention); [Creator Hub: Engagement](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/engagement)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, Oct 2026] "Improved Discovery Measurement in Creator Analytics": from **12 Oct 2026** impressions, clicks and plays from Home, Search and Charts are recorded via a more reliable pipeline (fewer events lost on app close/crash). Expect **higher impression/click/play counts and a small drop in PTR**; measurement-only, RFY ranking unchanged. Pre/post-12-Oct numbers are not directly comparable. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/improved-discovery-measurement-in-creator-analytics/4930122)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, 2023 — older] Home Recommendations conversion rate and icon-update annotations were first added to analytics in late 2023. — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-view-home-recommendations-conversion-rate-and-recent-icon-updates/2731683)
- [3P, unverified] "Around 2–2.4% QPTR is typical and top games reach ~3.6%". — [qptr.io blog](https://qptr.io/blog/roblox-thumbnail-ctr-tips)

## 3. Icon & thumbnail optimisation: official guidance, personalisation/experimentation, developer best practices, what is disallowed

### Takeaway
Officially, the thumbnail (16:9, 1920×1080) is the Home-tile creative and since Oct 2026 a custom one is mandatory for RFY; Roblox's native experimentation tool is **thumbnail personalisation** (2–5 active thumbnails, multi-armed bandit per user group; +8.5% average qPTR in tests, +12% average reported by staff in Feb 2025), not a classic A/B test, and there is no native icon test. Developer best practices (single focal subject, saturated colour, minimal text, test at tile size) are third-party/folklore; Roblox's own rules stress accuracy and uniqueness and penalise mismatch, giveaway bait and look-alikes.

### Cited Findings

**Official specs and rules**
- [DOC] Icon: square, **512×512** template; may scale down to ~150×150 so preview at small sizes; must be unique and show what users can expect; "ambiguous graphics" cause confusion; use colour to signal mood (bright saturated for fantasy/dreamy, muted for sombre, heavy contrast for horror); must pass moderation; **one icon per locale** (i.e. icons can be localised). — [Creator Hub: Experience icons](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/experience-icons)
- [DOC] Thumbnails: "16:9 aspect ratio and ideally 1920×1080 pixels"; formats .jpg/.gif/.png/.tga/.bmp; up to **10 images or videos** on the experience details page; alt text supported; free. Personalisation uploads <3 MB. — [Creator Hub: Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)
- [DOC] Video thumbnails: free, **quota 3 uploads/month** (rejections count); once approved the video appears first on the details page and "can also appear on the Home page"; not supported on Xbox, PlayStation, VR; all videos reviewed. Allowed: varied camera angles of real gameplay, minor post-processing, logo overlay, curated highlight sequences, in-game UI text, Roblox-catalogue music. **Prohibited:** misrepresented gameplay (e.g. roleplay game marketed as racing), artificially enhanced visuals, real-life footage/external overlays, spoken audio/voice-over/lyrics, promotional text or subjective claims (e.g. "50% off", "Free UGC!"); overlay text may only describe gameplay ("Collect coins to boost jumps"). — [Creator Hub: Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)
- [DOC] Official thumbnail best practices: avoid essential text/elements at the **bottom** (player-count overlay covers it); sharp 16:9 images; imagery unique and relevant to what players will find; video must show actual gameplay "not an exaggerated version"; use theme/colour to signal tone (bright for comical, dark for horror). — [Creator Hub: Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)
- [DOC] Discovery-quality rules affecting creatives: use original imagery and names, "avoid repetitive titles and previously published images"; thumbnails must reflect the game; "highly mismatched metadata and content is not recommended and is less visible in search" (example: "The Great Dinosaur Quest" with dinosaur thumbnails but generic gameplay); games whose metadata/place files closely resemble existing games "are no longer prioritized and may rank lower in search". A daily banner in the Creator Dashboard flags reduced exposure; appeal via Support > "Discovery Content Quality". — [Creator Hub: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] Low-quality detection uses "objective measures, models, and human reviews"; among indistinguishable games, the one that *first added the unique metadata or place file* is favoured (not the one created first). Reusing your own old icons/thumbnails across your games/groups is allowed and "shouldn't hurt discoverability". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] Ads Manager can generate **AI thumbnails** (three variations per text description); up to 10 thumbnails per campaign, distributed evenly. — [Creator Hub: Ads Manager](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager)

**Thumbnail personalisation (the official experimentation feature)**
- [DOC] Shows "the most relevant thumbnail to each user on the Home page" to improve qPTR; starts "only … when you activate 2 or more thumbnails"; **2–5 active thumbnails**; each is first shown to a random set of users, then the winner *per user group* gets more impressions while others keep minimal traffic. Testing result: "an average increase of +8.5%" in qPTR, some games +50%. Reporting ("Thumbnail Performance", populated "after a few hours"): Status, Impressions, Qualified Plays, Average Playtime, Qualified Play Through Rate, **Winning Segment**; charts of impressions and qPTR by thumbnail. Guidance: keep multiple thumbnails active rather than picking one winner; "test several new thumbnails with major updates, then leave them unchanged until the next update". — [Creator Hub: Thumbnails](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/thumbnails)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, 2024 — launch history] Tested on Home from July 2024; "[Live now] Personalize your thumbnails to attract more users" (late 2024): "Personalization uses multi-arm bandit (instead of A/B testing) to help you find multiple winning thumbnails in hours and to respect each user group's preferences"; managed in Creator Hub > Creations > Places > Thumbnails > Home Page tab. — [DevForum: Testing Personalized Thumbnails](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/testing-personalized-thumbnails-in-home-this-july/3039419); [DevForum: Live now](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/live-now-personalize-your-thumbnails-to-attract-more-users/3257233); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/roblox-will-let-game-devs-personalize-thumbnails-to-attract-more-players/)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, Feb 2025] "5 Tips From Roblox Staff…": over **8,000 experiences** adopted the feature; average **+12% relative lift in qPTR**, some +56%. Tips retrieved: (1) avoid restarting tests within 7 days — "frequent thumbnail restarts can prevent our model from accurately identifying the best performing thumbnails"; the best thumbnail can change over time; a temporary qPTR dip can follow a surge in Home recommendations; keep "a diverse set of thumbnails set to active"; "a fresh thumbnail alongside new gameplay significantly boosts discoverability and signals an actively maintained experience". (Tips 2–5 not fully retrieved.) — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/5-tips-from-roblox-staff-to-get-the-most-out-of-thumbnail-personalization/3471689); [Bloxy News on X](https://x.com/Bloxy_News/status/1890113378766704674)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, 2024 — older] Since April 2024 Home tiles use 16:9 thumbnails (first 16:9 image in the asset list); the default thumbnail "may lower the overall end-to-end conversion rate of your Home recommendations". — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/169-experience-thumbnails-are-coming-to-home-in-april-%E2%80%93-update-yours-now/2875237)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, March 2026] Video previews on Home tiles: test in Standout Games showed "+47% quality plays, +1.09% playtime", then expanded into RFY. — [Weekly Recap / How we are improving Home](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-we-are-improving-home-this-year/4502571)

**Developer / third-party best practices (NOT official)**
- [3P] Single focal point — "one character, one object, one moment"; bold saturated colours with strong subject/background separation; face-forward emotional close-up as one variant to test; show a reaction rather than the payoff; keep text out of the bottom band; judge at real card size. — [qptr.io](https://qptr.io/blog/roblox-thumbnail-ctr-tips); [gmmarket.me](https://gmmarket.me/community/post/how-to-optimize-your-roblox-game-icon-and-thumbnails-for-maximum-ctr); [vizzbees](https://vizzbees.com/blog/how-to-make-a-roblox-thumbnail)
- [3P] A study claiming to analyse 905 games' thumbnails exists (results not retrieved). — [clipclip.io](https://clipclip.io/guides/roblox-thumbnail-ctr)
- [FOLKLORE] A forum commenter claims bright blue skies raised CTR in their own ad tests. — [DevForum thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-can-i-improve-my-horrible-play-through-rate/3524823)
- [3P, conflicts with Roblox] One blog claims Roblox has "native A/B testing for thumbnails"; Roblox's own wording is that personalisation uses a multi-armed bandit *instead of* A/B testing. — [rowatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/roblox-thumbnail-a-b-testing-what-the-data-actually-shows) vs [DevForum: Live now](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/live-now-personalize-your-thumbnails-to-attract-more-users/3257233)

### Inferences
- Since PTR is a top-tier RFY signal *and* bounce rate (<60 s) is a top-tier negative signal, a clickbait creative that raises PTR but mismatches gameplay is self-defeating: it lifts one signal while worsening another and risks the "mismatched metadata" exposure reduction.
- The practical workflow implied by the official guidance: ship 3–5 distinct concepts with each major update, let the bandit run ≥7 days, read "Winning Segment" to learn which audience each creative wins, and refresh only with the next major update.
- Icons matter mainly on Charts, Search, Continue and Friends surfaces (square tiles), while the 16:9 thumbnail is the RFY creative; the two therefore need separate optimisation.

### Gaps
- No public, verifiable A/B result tables from named studios were found; "update the icon with every update", "UPDATE/NEW badges", "show the rarest item", seasonal variants and GFX-style preferences are common practice on top charts but I found **no official statement or controlled data** supporting them (and found no source explicitly recommending a new icon per update).
- No native icon A/B test or icon personalisation was found as of Nov 2026 (only a 2021 feature request). Localised-thumbnail behaviour on Home is unclear (a 2025 bug thread reports localised thumbnails not showing on Home).
- Tips 2–5 of the staff post and the 905-game study contents were not retrievable.

## 4. Title & description optimisation, search ranking, localisation, genre taxonomy, maturity labels and age gating

### Takeaway
Roblox search is officially "semantic" across supported languages and penalises irrelevant keywords, giveaway-led titles, mismatched metadata and look-alike titles; bracket/emoji tags are a pervasive convention but have no official endorsement. Reach is structurally limited by genre accuracy, maturity label and—since 2026—by the Roblox Kids (5–8) / Select (9–15) account system, whose "Highly Engaged Player" entry threshold (500 → 250 → planned 100 in Nov 2026) decides whether under-16 audiences can see a game at all.

### Cited Findings

**Search and metadata (official)**
- [DOC] Search was "historically relevance-based on exact queries and limited metadata such as titles"; it "now supports semantic search for all officially supported languages", so natural queries like "food games" work. — [Creator Hub: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] Best practices: "avoid irrelevant keywords in metadata"; titles/descriptions must reflect the actual game; "don't rely on promotional monetary rewards"; use original names, avoid repetitive titles; add your own spin on trends in titles, images, descriptions and content. **Leading with giveaways** (example title: "Robux! Play now!") is "not prioritized". Mismatched and non-unique games are "less visible in search"/"may rank lower in search". Roblox "continually reclassifies content quality with every update". — [Creator Hub: Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] The similarity test: "whether a user searching would see multiple games with indistinguishable visuals and titles". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] The "Search" acquisition source also includes "Recommended Experiences on Experience Details" (similar-games rail). — [Creator Hub: Acquisition](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)
- [DOC] Search-ad auction reveals that Roblox scores "each game's relevance to the query" using player, ad and query information; clickbait/spam games are blocked from search ads. For an exact game-name query, the exact match is the large primary tile and ads come after it. — [Creator Hub: Search ads](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads)
- [Official statement quoted second-hand, 2023 — older] Search results "consider user account information, activities, relevance to the search and Experience content, quality, performance, and popularity". — quoted in [DevForum community research post](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/my-research-on-the-roblox-algorithm-discovery-for-experiencesgames/2707618)

### Inferences
- For a new game in late 2026, the largest single "reach multiplier" in metadata is not keywords but eligibility: maturity label ≤ Mild/Moderate plus passing the Kids/Select selection (HEP threshold or expedited review), because under-16 accounts otherwise cannot see the game on any surface.
- Bracketed tags ("[UPDATE]", "[🎃EVENT]", "[x2 LUCK]") function as tile-level CTR copy, not as search keywords; Roblox's rules only prohibit them when they lead with monetary giveaways or mislead. Tags that promise rewards ("FREE ROBUX/UGC") collide with the "leading with giveaways" rule.
- Semantic search reduces the value of keyword stuffing in descriptions (and "irrelevant keywords" is an explicit negative), so descriptions should be accurate, localised, and oriented to conversion on the details page (what the game is, update log, social links) rather than keyword lists.

### Gaps
- No official statement found on emoji/bracket tags, on description update logs/codes/group prompts affecting ranking, or on how auto-translation quantitatively affects reach.
- Could not open the Content maturity doc or the Kids/Select DevForum posts in full: exact current HEP figure (250 vs 100) on 17 Nov 2026, the three selection steps, and the precise label-to-age matrix should be verified in Creator Hub > Audience Reach before publication.
- No official 2026 description of the search ranking model beyond "semantic search" + quality rules; the detailed factor list dates from 2023.

## 5. Other acquisition levers: ads, events, notifications, social graph, creators, off-platform funnels, cross-promotion

### Takeaway
Paid and external traffic do not count toward RFY signals (official), so ads/TikTok serve to seed players, CCU, friends-graph spread, Kids/Select eligibility and Creator Rewards income rather than to "train" the algorithm; Roblox gives no official cost-per-play benchmark and third-party figures diverge by two orders of magnitude. Roblox pays creators directly for off-platform acquisition (35% of a new/reactivated user's first $100 of purchases over 60 days), and 2025's mega-hits were driven largely by TikTok/YouTube virality.

### Cited Findings

**Ads Manager (official, current)**
- [DOC] Objectives: **Plays** (players most likely to start a session), **Earnings (Limited)** (most likely to spend Robux; ROAS reporting), **Engagement** (HEP for Kids/Select). Auto-bidding: set budget and duration, Roblox calculates the bid. CPP = spend ÷ plays. Campaigns show "automatically … on both the Home page and in search results". Audiences: All, **New** (never played or not in 180+ days), **Recent** (last 30 days; needs 10,000+ recent players), **Lapsed** (30–180 days; needs 20,000+). Advanced targeting: location, age, gender, genre, device. Reporting: spend, impressions, clicks, plays, CPP, playtime, earnings; views for All/New/Recent/Resurrected users (7D, 30D); attribution up to 30 days after the ad; data delay up to 48 h. Requirements: 13+, verified email; card payments 18+; non-unique games may get "no ad spend and no conversions". Ad moderation ~24 h. No minimum campaign budget stated. — [Creator Hub: Ads Manager](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/ads-manager)
- [DOC] **Search ads**: up to 10 exact-match keywords per ad set; second-price auction with relevance-adjusted eCPM (winner pays second price adjusted + $0.01; example: $12 bid vs $10 bid pays $10.01); appear at top of search results (after an exact-name match); 13+ only, no audience targeting; keyword-level reporting requires one keyword per ad set. — [Creator Hub: Search ads](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/search-ads)
- [DOC] Ads and RFY: "RFY is separate from ads"; ad-acquired users' engagement, retention and monetisation are **excluded** from RFY signals, even if they later return via Home. After an ad burst, RFY impressions can dip because ad-acquired users "are already considered acquired" and generally won't be shown the game in RFY again — "this isn't a penalty". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DEVFORUM-snippet, 2023 — older] Sponsored Experiences moved into Ads Manager; billed per play ("a player joining your experience after landing on the experience details page"). — [DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/sponsored-experiences-moving-to-ads-manager/2661756)
- [3P, mutually inconsistent — treat as unreliable] Cost per play: "$0.45 average, 1.2% CTR" (benchmarks page claiming 850+ promoted games) vs "$0.01–0.05 per visit" vs "$0.006–0.008 a play, worst $0.024" (developer dashboards) vs "$0.12 per player" (one simulator case). Ad credit price also disputed (one guide: 1 credit ≈ 263 Robux ≈ $1). — [bloxg benchmarks](https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-advertising-benchmarks); [bloxg guide](https://bloxg.com/guides/roblox-ads-guide); [clipclip.io](https://clipclip.io/guides/roblox-ads-cost); [metaclip](https://metaclip.app/guides/roblox-game-marketing-cost)
- [3P] "If your Day 1 retention is below 25%, paid acquisition is probably making your game worse"; wait for ≥2 weeks of organic data before trusting retention. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/day-1-retention-thresholds-before-you-buy-roblox-ads)
- [NEWSROOM/press-snippet, 2026] Policy shifts: from 4 May 2026 all brand deals must be registered with Roblox before launch; a revenue share on creator–brand deals takes effect January 2027 (percentage not yet public). Pre-roll video ads during loading announced at RDC26. — [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/03/21/roblox-to-overhaul-ad-policies-introduce-revenue-sharing-in-2027); [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/roblox-to-take-cut-of-creator-brand-deal-revenue-starting-2027/); [DevForum: RDC26](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880)

**Events, updates, notifications (official)**
- [DOC] Experience Events: appear on the details page (Join Event / **Notify Me**), a shareable event page, the group's Events tab, and Charts' "Trending Events in Experiences" (≥1,000 RSVPs, started ≤7 days ago, public; ordered by DAU). Max **10** ongoing/upcoming events. RSVP'd users get a stream notification (and optional push) at start. Join attribution via `GameJoinContext`/`GetJoinData()`. **Off-Platform Featuring**: submit ≥7 days ahead; selection considers "performance signals (DAU growth, playtime, retention, monetization), current trends, and seasonal fit"; free if selected. — [Creator Hub: Experience events](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-events)
- [DOC] Experience Notifications: "delivered to opted-in users age 13+ through their Roblox notification stream"; opt-in prompt not shown to under-13s, already-opted users, or users who saw it in the past 30 days; prompt text standardised; personalised templates with parameters; optional launch data (≤200 bytes) to deep-link the joiner; analytics for reachable audience and performance. The Discovery doc lists notifications (milestones, friend activity) and in-game permission prompts as the tool to "bring back lapsed users". — [Creator Hub: Experience notifications](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/experience-notifications); [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] Details page hygiene counts as an acquisition lever: keep events, Roblox Group, passes and subscriptions current; add images and videos. — [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery); [Acquisition](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)

**Off-platform funnels, creators and Creator Rewards (official)**
- [DOC] Roblox's own advice for external acquisition: post regularly on social accounts linked from the details page; build and engage a community; "partner with creators and influencers"; use **share links** to track off-platform acquisition (share-link table: qualified plays, 7-day playtime, D7 retention, 30-day revenue per user). — [Creator Hub: Acquisition](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)
- [DOC] **Creator Rewards** (replaced Engagement-Based Payouts and the Creator Affiliate Program on **24 July 2025**): (a) *Daily Engagement*: **5 Robux per Active Spender per day** who spends ≥10 minutes in the experience and for whom it is one of the **first three experiences launched that day** (Active Spender = ≥$9.99 qualifying purchases in the last 60 days, on platform ≥60 days). (b) *Audience Expansion*: **35% revenue share on the first $100** of a New or Reactivated (60+ days lapsed) user's qualifying purchases anywhere on Roblox in their first 60 days, when the user arrives via the creator's share link, direct link, or by searching the experience's specific name as first session that day, plays 10+ minutes that day, and the experience averages 100+ DAU; requires ID-verified account. Video Stars members can earn both. — [Creator Hub: Creator Rewards](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards)
- [DOC — deprecated] Former Creator Affiliate terms (up to 50% of a new user's Robux purchases for six months, capped at $100) are historical. — [Creator Hub: Affiliates](https://create.roblox.com/docs/en-us/affiliates)
- [3P, Naavik 2026] Growth of Grow a Garden, Steal a Brainrot and 99 Nights in the Forest "was driven largely by YouTube and TikTok influencers, where one video can push a game to millions of concurrent users"; Brainrot's success drew on "mass viral popularity on TikTok and off-platform amplification through 'clipping'". — [Naavik: The State of UGC Games (2026)](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/the-state-of-ugc-games-2026/)
- [NEWSROOM-snippet, Dec 2025] Roblox claims that in July–August 2025 "one in every five game-related videos posted on TikTok globally mentioned Roblox" (company claim). — [Roblox newsroom: 2025 Replay](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/12/roblox-replay-decoded-search-style)
- [3P] Virality decays fast: experiences "go viral, spike into the millions, and then vanish"; Grow a Garden (first game past 20M CCU) was at roughly 1–1.2M CCU by May 2026; Steal a Brainrot (only game past 25M CCU) cited at ~500–550K average by one tracker. — [Joost van Dreunen, "The Brainrot economy"](https://superjoost.substack.com/p/the-brainrot-economy); [Wikipedia: Steal a Brainrot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_a_Brainrot); [Wikipedia: Grow a Garden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grow_a_Garden)
- [DOC] Cross-promotion inside a studio's portfolio is tracked as "Teleport" (traffic from other games via teleporting) and "Portal ads" sources in Acquisition analytics. Manipulating teleports to inflate engagement is prohibited under Creator Rewards anti-abuse rules. — [Acquisition](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition); [Creator Rewards](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards)

### Inferences
- How external virality becomes "algorithmic lift" (mechanism inferred from official rules, not stated by Roblox): TikTok/ads users are excluded from RFY signals, but (1) retrieval needs only a minimum number of plays "from any source"; (2) external players raise CCU (Top Playing Now), revenue (Top Earning), event RSVPs/DAU (Trending Events) and search volume for the game name; (3) they expand the friends graph — friend joins/invites surface the game in Friends activity and generate intentional co-play among RFY-acquired users; (4) RFY's explore phase then tests the game on organic cohorts whose behaviour *does* count. A "direct growth" signal (test, RDC 2026) would additionally reward games that bring brand-new users to the platform.
- Ads are therefore rational for: getting past the retrieval minimum, filling servers at launch, reaching the Kids/Select HEP threshold (Engagement objective), reactivating lapsed players (Lapsed audience) and gathering FTUE data — not for boosting RFY rank. The "ads kick-start the algorithm" folklore is only indirectly true.
- Creator Rewards changes the economics of influencer seeding: a studio's share link in a TikTok/YouTube bio converts new/reactivated users into a 35%-of-first-$100 revenue stream, and the Daily Engagement rule pays for being one of a spender's *first three* experiences of the day with ≥10 minutes — an incentive aligned with daily-return design.

### Gaps
- No official or reliable cost-per-play benchmark for 2026; third-party numbers range from ~$0.006 to $0.45 per play and cannot be reconciled.
- No source quantifies what share of a viral game's traffic comes from Home vs external, nor an official statement on TikTok → RFY lift. Roblox's Portal ads doc and any "Star Creators"/Video Stars seeding programme details were not retrieved (Video Stars is only mentioned inside the Creator Rewards doc).
- Experience-notification rate limits and usage guidelines sit in doc includes that were not retrieved. ## 6. How game design feeds the algorithm's metrics (and soft-launch KPI thresholds)

### Takeaway
Roblox's own docs tie design directly to the signals: get players to fun within 5 minutes (bounce rate is measured at <60 s and 61–180 s), build progression for D7, ship small updates every 2–4 weeks and big ones every 2–3 months for D30, design for group play (intentional co-play days is a named signal), and keep performance solid across devices. Roblox's official sequencing is "fix retention first, then monetisation"; numeric soft-launch gates exist only as conflicting third-party rules of thumb.

### Cited Findings

**Official design guidance mapped to signals**
- [DOC] Priority order: "Focus on core gameplay, retention, and accurate metadata first. If retention is low, fix gameplay first. Once retention is strong, improve monetization." A strong D8–28 signal "is unlikely if Day 1 and Day 2–7 are poor", so work stage by stage — "especially PTR, bounce rate, playtime, and play days". — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq)
- [DOC] First session / bounce: "Make sure users have fun within their first five minutes"; get users into the fun "ideally in 5 minutes or less"; brief tutorial or contextual tooltips, "avoid long tutorials"; consider starter items and currency; deliver "a joyful moment after the first core loop completion"; preview future progress; instrument each FTUE step and fix the biggest drop-offs. — [Creator Hub: Engagement](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/engagement); [Creator Hub: Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)
- [DOC] Bounce rate is evaluated in two buckets — under 60 seconds and 61–180 seconds — as a top-tier negative signal. — [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] D1 levers: clear and fun core loop, balanced challenge, rewards on completion (feedback, currency, items, achievements), FTUE, performance (test across devices, monitor crash rate/frame rate/errors with each update). D7 levers: progression with short- and long-term goals, content variety (modes, items, social features), balanced pacing, measure progression reach. D30 levers: "smaller updates to existing mechanics every **2–4 weeks**, and larger feature updates every **2–3 months**", plus social mechanics — trading, parties, guilds, PvP, tournaments, leaderboards. — [Creator Hub: Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention)
- [DOC] Session time: "remove unnecessary barriers to social interaction, and design for group play where possible"; keep enough content available up front for the first session; "limit excessive textures, meshes, and other variables that could slow the game". Average session time is "closely tied to retention". — [Creator Hub: Engagement](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/engagement)
- [DOC] Co-play: "Intentional co-play days per user" counts unique days users return to play *with friends* via join, invite and private servers. — [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] Playtime credit is capped at 60 minutes per user per game per day; play days and spend days count unique days. — [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery)
- [DOC] Updates and the explore phase: a content update can trigger a spike of new users from recommendations (explore); if that cohort engages and monetises, distribution expands. Roblox staff: "a fresh thumbnail alongside new gameplay significantly boosts discoverability and signals an actively maintained experience". — [Discovery](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery); [DevForum: 5 Tips From Roblox Staff](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/5-tips-from-roblox-staff-to-get-the-most-out-of-thumbnail-personalization/3471689)
- [DOC] Monetisation signals are frequency- and depth-based (spend days per user, Robux per user) and are part of RFY; games strong in both retention and monetisation "may get broader distribution". Older acquisition doc wording (still live) frames the algorithm as using session time and D1 retention ("how engaging") plus payer conversion and ARPPU ("how invested"), compared with similar games. — [Discovery FAQ](https://create.roblox.com/docs/discovery-faq); [Acquisition](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/acquisition)
- [NEWSROOM-snippet, 2026] Roblox leadership framing: quick-click games that lack depth were displacing games "players value for the long term"; ranking now looks to "the first 28 days and beyond" and is age-aware (younger players favour shorter games, older players deeper ones). — [Roblox newsroom, June 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox); [Roblox newsroom, RDC 2026](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/09/rdc-2026-the-world-needs-more-play)
- [DOC] Creator Rewards adds a monetary incentive for the same behaviours: 10+ minute sessions from paying users, being among the first three experiences launched in a day. — [Creator Hub: Creator Rewards](https://create.roblox.com/docs/creator-rewards)
- [DEVFORUM/roadmap-snippet] Friction reducers on the way: browser play with no install and standalone apps ("Roblox Everywhere", late 2026); passes/developer products surfaced on Home before entry (late 2026). — [DevForum: RDC26](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/rdc26-what-we-announced/4865880); [Creator Roadmap Fall Update](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creator-roadmap-2026-fall-update/4880208)

**Soft-launch / scaling thresholds (third-party, conflicting — not official)**
- [3P] Roblox-specific D1 ladder: "Good = 20%, Great = 30%, Excellent = 40%+". — [bloxg](https://bloxg.com/guides/roblox-player-retention)
- [3P] D1 below 25% → do not buy ads; collect ≥2 weeks of organic data first. — [RoWatcher](https://rowatcher.com/news/day-1-retention-thresholds-before-you-buy-roblox-ads)
- [3P, Jan 2025 — older] ~12% D1 cited as a target (matches the *median* of Roblox's own benchmark example rather than a high bar). — [Kushal Writes](https://kushaltimsina.com/blog/2025/01/22/understanding-the-roblox-algorithm-to-grow-your-game/)
- [DOC, for calibration] Official illustrative similar-games D1 range: 12.11% (50th percentile) to 18.73% (90th). — [Analytics dashboard](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/analytics-dashboard)
- [3P, mobile-industry framing, not Roblox] D1 >35–40%, D7 >15% as global-launch gates — do not transfer directly to Roblox. — [galaxy4games](https://galaxy4games.com/en/knowledgebase/blog/game-launch-strategy-in-2026-from-aso-and-ad-creatives-to-launch-ready-kpis-ua-scaling-and-publishers)

### Inferences
- Design checklist implied by the Nov 2026 signal set: (1) time-to-fun under ~60 s and certainly under 3 min (the two bounce buckets); (2) a reason to return on *separate days* during days 2–7 and 8–28 (daily rewards, timed crops/eggs, rotating shops, streaks) because play days outrank raw minutes and minutes are capped at 60/day; (3) friend-facing mechanics that create *intentional* joins (party boosts, trading, co-op bonuses, private-server value) to feed co-play days; (4) low-priced repeatable purchases that create multiple *spend days* rather than one large purchase; (5) updates every 2–4 weeks paired with fresh thumbnails to re-enter the explore phase; (6) low-end mobile performance, since crashes and slow loads show up as <60 s bounces.
- "Fill servers and matchmaking" and "fast load times" are not named by Roblox as ranking inputs, but they act through bounce rate, first-session retention and co-play; the docs' only explicit technical advice is device testing and limiting heavy assets.
- A defensible soft-launch gate using only official instruments: before scaling spend, be at or above the 90th-percentile edge of the "similar experiences" range for PTR, D1, D7 and average session time in Creator Analytics, with the under-60-second bounce trending down — rather than any fixed universal percentage.

### Gaps
- No Roblox staff statement with numeric soft-launch thresholds (D1/D7/session/CPP) was found; all numeric gates are third-party and inconsistent.
- No official 2026 statement on server size/fill, matchmaking, load time or playtime-reward/AFK mechanics as ranking factors (note the 60-minute daily playtime cap limits the value of AFK farming for RFY).
- Named-studio postmortems (Gamefam, Supersocial, Voldex, Twin Atlas, Do Big Studios, etc.) and RDC 2025/2026 session talks on growth were not retrieved in this pass (network restrictions and search budget); the writer should treat design-to-metric links above as Roblox-documented guidance plus inference, not as studio-verified case data.
