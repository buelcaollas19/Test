> **Method caveat for the report writer.** Page fetching was blocked in this research session (every non-search host failed DNS resolution), so ALL figures below come from web-search result summaries/snippets, not from full-page reads. ## 1. Top Roblox experiences by CCU now (late 2026) and across 2025–2026; peak records and dates

### Takeaway
The all-time records were all set in the Aug–Oct 2025 "viral summer" (Steal a Brainrot ~25.8M, Grow a Garden ~22.3M, 99 Nights in the Forest ~14.2M, platform ~47.3M CCU); in late 2026 the #1 game is a new mid-2026 hit, **Steal An Egg** (launched late July 2026, ~14.2–14.3M peak in Sept 2026, ~0.5–0.8M CCU in Oct–Nov 2026), while the 2025 record-holders have decayed by more than 99% from peak and evergreens (Brookhaven, Murder Mystery 2, Adopt Me, Blox Fruits, RIVALS, 99 Nights) fill the rest of the top 10.

## 2. Which genres dominate by CCU/plays and by revenue

### Takeaway
By plays, Simulation (which on Roblox absorbs incremental, tycoon, farming/idle, pet-collection and "steal" games) is by far the largest genre (~37% of plays, Aug–Sep 2026), followed by Survival (~15%), Obby/Platformer, Roleplay & Avatar Sim and Action (each ~8%); by revenue, modelled estimates put Simulation first, then Roleplay & Avatar Sim and Survival, with an FPS (RIVALS) and the simulator Steal An Egg alternating as the top monthly earner in 2026. No official Roblox genre-level revenue or hours breakdown was found.

### Inferences
- Genre mapping for the report (my synthesis from the above): the highest-CCU *spikes* come from Simulation hybrids (idle farming, steal/base-raid, pet hatch/collect); the most *durable* CCU comes from roleplay/life-sim (Brookhaven, Adopt Me), social deduction (Murder Mystery 2), anime RPG (Blox Fruits), fashion (Dress to Impress) and competitive FPS (RIVALS); survival co-op (99 Nights, Forsaken, Dead Rails) sits between the two.
- High CCU does not equal high revenue: an FPS with ~150–400K CCU (RIVALS) is estimated to out-earn roleplay games with 2–3x its CCU, and Roblox itself said 2025's viral games monetised much better per hour than the 2026 mix (see section 3).

### Gaps
- No official Roblox genre-share data (hours or bookings by genre) and no Roblox genre-taxonomy document was retrievable; Dubit/RoWatcher categories are tracker taxonomies.
- No reliable genre-level figures for tower defense/gacha, RNG, obby/tower or sports sub-genres specifically; no revenue estimates found for Anime Vanguards, Blue Lock Rivals, Pet Simulator 99, Dress to Impress, Adopt Me.

## 3. Roblox platform-level stats for 2026 (DAU, bookings, hours, demographics, regions, devices)

### Takeaway
2026 is a hangover year after 2025's record: Q3 2026 had 138M DAU (−9% YoY), ~34bn hours (−15% YoY) and US$1.70bn bookings (−12% YoY), with Roblox attributing the decline to tough comparisons against 2025's viral hits, age-check/chat headwinds, weaker under-13 monetisation, and a deliberate June 2026 recommendation change favouring 28-day retention over short-term monetisation; the audience keeps ageing (18+ ≈ 27–29% of users) and APAC is now the largest region.

## 4. How a niche saturates: clone waves and decay curves

### Takeaway
Clone waves on Roblox now arrive within days of (sometimes before) a breakout, the genre leader keeps the overwhelming majority of the niche's players (one count: 105 "Steal a ___" clones with the original holding 92% of players), and even leaders decay brutally — Grow a Garden and Steal a Brainrot both lost >99% of peak CCU within ~12 months — while simulators/tycoons decay fastest and social/fashion/roleplay formats hold longest.

- [PRESS] Event-driven spikes are extremely transient: Grow a Garden fell from a 21.3M spike to 7.6M within an hour of an update event (PocketGamer observation via DEV Community) — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/wlz/why-grow-a-garden-and-steal-a-brainrot-bet-on-boring-idle-tech-the-luau-loop-behind-2djf)

### Inferences
- Saturation pattern (synthesis): (1) breakout with a simple, readable loop → (2) dozens-to-thousands of reskins within days/weeks using the "[Verb] a [Noun]" title formula → (3) winner-take-most: the original keeps ~90%+ of niche players → (4) leader decays 50% within ~2 months and 95–99% within 6–12 months → (5) the theme (e.g. brainrot) survives by migrating into new mechanics (escape, obby, tower defense) rather than through the original loop.
- The next mega-hit tends to be a recombination of the previous two (Steal An Egg = Steal a Brainrot's theft loop + pet-egg hatching/collection), not a clone of one of them.
- Copycats win only when they change the accessibility/skill curve (Fish It vs Fisch), not when they merely reskin.

### Gaps
- No quantified data found on the 2021–22 pet-simulator clone wave after Pet Simulator X, the 2024 RNG clone wave after Sol's RNG, the "Grow a ___" clone count, or Dead Rails clones. These waves are widely discussed in the community but I found no citable numbers; treat as qualitative only.
- Dates of the RoWatcher brainrot snapshots are unclear.

## 5. Breakouts through hybridisation: what the novel twist was (plus historical genre-defining hits 2017–2024)

### Takeaway
Every 2025–2026 mega-hit combined a familiar low-friction core loop with one borrowed system from another genre — idle farming + offline growth + social trading (Grow a Garden), tycoon collection + PvP theft + meme characters (Steal a Brainrot), co-op survival + party-based progression (99 Nights in the Forest), theft + pet-egg gacha (Steal An Egg) — whereas the historical genre-definers (Jailbreak, Adopt Me!, Dress to Impress, Blox Fruits, Sol's RNG) each established a template that later hybrids recombined.

**Hybrid breakouts 2025–2026**

### Inferences
- The acquirer model (Voldex, DoBig, WAKA) means a breakout game has a realistic exit path within months; it also means incumbents in each niche are operated by well-funded live-ops teams, raising the bar for late entrants.
